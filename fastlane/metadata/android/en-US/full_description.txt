Auriqo is an Android music app for playback, local library use, downloads, lyrics, and optional music services.

Features:

* Stream and play supported music sources.
* Keep selected media available for offline playback.
* View synchronized lyrics where a lyric source provides them.
* Search, manage playlists, and use playback controls in the background.
* Identify music with the optional microphone-based recognition feature.
* Import supported playlists and connect optional services when you choose to do so.
* Choose FOSS or GMS distribution variants where they are made available by an authorized distributor.
* Review bundled open-source attribution notices without a runtime network request.

Privacy and service choices:

Some features make requests to content, lyric, recognition, translation, or optional account providers. The GMS variant includes Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics dependencies, with collection disabled by default and enabled only after an explicit recorded opt-in. The FOSS variant excludes those flavor-specific dependencies and telemetry controls. Review the distribution-specific privacy notice, requested permissions, and third-party terms before enabling optional services.
